 My Favorite Series: *Arcane*

Overview
*Arcane* is an animated series produced by **Fortiche Production** in collaboration with **Riot Games**, based on the universe of the game *League of Legends*. It bridges action, drama, and stunning visual storytelling, exploring the origin stories of iconic characters from Piltover and Zaun.

Why I Love It
- **Visual Style**: It blends 2D and 3D animation in a unique, painterly way that feels alive and atmospheric.
- **Storytelling**: The writing balances high-stakes political intrigue with deeply personal, emotional arcs about family, ambition, and belonging.
- **Characters**: The show gives depth to figures like Vi, Jinx, Caitlyn, and Viktor, showing how circumstances and choices shape who they become.
